# Code of conduct

Yucu is built by students of the Instituto Tecnológico de Tlaxiaco, for artisans and producers of the Mixteca. Everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, reviews, field work or chats) agrees to this code. It is based on the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our commitment

We want a welcoming space for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, language, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

* Be kind and patient, especially with people who are learning. Many contributors are students opening their first pull request.
* Review code, not people. Explain the reason behind a requested change.
* Accept feedback gracefully and admit mistakes.
* Write in Spanish or English; both are welcome. Prefer plain language.
* Respect the communities we work with: never publish names, voices, photos or locations of artisans, buyers or interviewees ([`PRIVACIDAD.md`](./docs/research/PRIVACIDAD.md)).

## Unacceptable behavior

* Harassment, insults, or discriminatory or sexualized comments.
* Mocking someone's level of experience, accent or language.
* Publishing someone's private information without explicit permission.
* Submitting work you do not understand as your own, or pressuring others to approve low-quality work.
* Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a classroom or a community assembly.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainer, [@josealfredo79](https://github.com/josealfredo79). For academic matters involving TecNM students, the institution's own rules also apply.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently block anyone who repeats or seriously breaks it. Reports are handled confidentially.
