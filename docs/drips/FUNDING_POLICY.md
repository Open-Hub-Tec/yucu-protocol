# Funding policy

This file says who receives money for work on Yucu Protocol, and who does not. It replaces `drips.config.json` and `.github/FUNDING.yml`, which were removed (see [What changed](#what-changed)).

## Drips Wave rewards

- Rewards for a Wave issue are paid **by Drips directly to the contributor** who solved it, under the [Drips Wave terms](https://docs.drips.network/wave/terms-and-rules) (age 18 or older, identity check). Yucu has no say in the amount: Drips sets it from the issue's complexity and its own rules.
- **Nobody else takes a share.** The maintainer, Open Hub TecNM and the repository do not receive, hold, pool or redistribute any part of a contributor's reward. There is no "splits" contract and no reserve fund.
- **Who writes code:** TecNM Campus Tlaxiaco students, and any contributor on issues labeled `open-to-all` (see [CONTRIBUTING](../../CONTRIBUTING.md)). The maintainer writes issues, reviews and merges pull requests, and does not solve Wave issues.
- **Taking part in Drips is voluntary.** A student's course grade never depends on applying to a Wave, passing the identity check or receiving a reward. A student who cannot or does not want to take part does the same course work and keeps the authorship of their commits.
- We do not promise scholarships, fixed amounts or payment dates.

## Conflict of interest

The maintainer of this repository also maintains [MicoPay](https://github.com/Micopay/micopay-protocol), another project in Drips Wave, and teaches the students who work on Yucu. Because the same person writes the issues, assigns them and grades the students, we state it here instead of leaving it for others to find. The rules above are how we limit it: the maintainer takes no share of any reward, no grade depends on Drips, and issues labeled `open-to-all` can be taken by contributors who are not the maintainer's students. Yucu and MicoPay are separate projects, with separate organizations and issues.

## Grants to the project

A grant to the project as a whole (for example, from the Stellar Community Fund) is a separate matter from Wave rewards. Before accepting one:

1. The budget is published in this file: what the money pays for, who receives it and how much.
2. It is agreed in writing with TecNM Campus Tlaxiaco.
3. Any payment to the maintainer for their own work is listed by name and amount. It never comes out of student rewards.

## What changed

On 2026-11-12 we removed two files that did not describe how Drips works and that made promises we cannot keep:

- `drips.config.json` described an 80 % / 20 % split of "the Drips fund" between students and the maintainer, a reserve fund for scholarships and a "Drips smart contract" that reassigns inactive students' shares. None of this exists: Drips does not read that file, and Wave pays each contributor directly. It also cited figures without evidence (60 students, a 50-artisan cohort), the wrong repository URL and a contract file that does not exist.
- `.github/FUNDING.yml` pointed the "Sponsor" button to the maintainer's personal account and to a Drips link that did not work.

## Resumen en español

- En Drips Wave, **Drips le paga directamente a quien resolvió la issue**. Ni el docente ni Open Hub ni el repositorio reciben, guardan o reparten parte de ese pago.
- Solo programan los estudiantes del TecNM Campus Tlaxiaco. El docente redacta issues, revisa y fusiona.
- Participar en Drips es voluntario: la calificación no depende de postularse, pasar la verificación de identidad ni cobrar.
- No prometemos becas, montos ni fechas.
- El docente también mantiene MicoPay, otro proyecto en Drips Wave, y califica a los estudiantes que trabajan en Yucu. Lo decimos aquí para que nadie tenga que descubrirlo; las reglas de arriba son cómo lo limitamos.
- Si algún día llega un fondo para el proyecto (por ejemplo, del SCF), su presupuesto se publica aquí y se acuerda por escrito con el TecNM antes de aceptarlo.
